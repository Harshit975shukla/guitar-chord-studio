Experimental Electric Lab — owner-authorized model preview
========================================================

Scope
  The integrated Guitar Lab's electric choice uses the supplied recovered APK
  model. The separate electric-lab.html sandbox page was removed on
  5 October 2026. The model does not replace the Chord Player or native
  acoustic guitar. Only the integrated Lab's electric choice is replaced.
  Its original steel-string acoustic and classical models remain unchanged.
  Geometry is verified; the original runtime finish is NOT yet verified.
  The owner supplied this APK as their own app and authorized converted models.
  No broader redistribution licence is inferred. The owner approved this
  page's deployment to their backup and live sites on 4 October 2026.

Presentation and demonstration
  (The finish choice, wireframe, inspection areas, teaching strings and
  playback below belonged to the removed sandbox page; the Lab uses only the
  fixed cherry finish and the shared renderer.)
  Runtime grouping rotates/recenters the model; source buffers stay intact.
  Cherry red, Midnight blue and Vintage cream are custom Lab finishes.
  Runtime material groups separate paint, maple-coloured neck/headstock,
  rosewood-coloured fretboard, nickel-coloured hardware, dark pickups/knobs
  and light inlays. Connectivity and local geometry define the masks;
  source vertices, normals, UVs, index order and the logo are not edited.
  These colours are an interpretation, not recovered APK textures or proof
  of actual wood species/hardware materials. Recovered neutral restores the
  saved source material. An owned generated room environment provides studio
  reflections; it is not Cube_Sunol. Finish choice survives model retries.
  Optional wireframe uses the existing triangle topology.
  Named inspection areas and a marker navigate one intact source mesh;
  they are not recovered separable component groups.
  Separate gold teaching strings and fret dots follow standard-tuning chord
  shapes. Their visibly slowed vibration is illustrative, not physical
  simulation or recovered APK motion. Animated APK hands are not included.
  Playback uses the existing locally hosted Musicca electric-guitar bank
  through the application's shared recording engine. It is not APK audio
  or a prediction of the recovered model's tone. The existing audio
  provenance remains under public/audio/musicca-guitar/.
  The Lab does not request microphone access or change app settings or
  main-player state. It retains its existing optional private
  recorded-guitar preview; that audio is not recovered APK audio.

Integrated assembly view
  The electric choice uses one fixed cherry finish inside the original Lab.
  Instrument selection, the disassembly slider and Assemble / Disassemble,
  component isolation/hover, cameras, turntable, 2D and audio preview remain.
  The acoustic and classical model/renderer paths are not replaced.
  The main primitive has 94 positional connected components. Analysis welds
  duplicated seam positions only to identify connectivity; it never changes
  vertex attributes. Eight groups contain every source triangle exactly once:
    Body/neck/headstock and strap hardware: 2,085, plus 6 original logo faces
    Fretboard, 24 fret wires and inlays: 293
    Nut, three clamps and screws: 762
    Six tuning machines: 744
    Two pickup housings: 20
    Bridge, saddles, screws and vibrato: 2,010
    Control knobs: 388
    Six original strings: 144
  The body, neck and headstock are one connected surface and stay together.
  Only whole connected components are grouped. In-memory subset index buffers
  retain original winding and relative face order, share unchanged position /
  normal / UV attributes, and preserve the original logo and finish roles.
  Reassembly restores zero offsets; the source GLB is not edited.
  Assembly-only custom materials display both sides of the recovered surfaces
  so the open neck shell stays visible when uncovered. This adds no faces and
  does not turn the source surface into a closed solid.
  Grouping, staggered movement and separation paths are authored educational
  illustrations, not recovered APK animation or a verified repair sequence.
  Physical joints, wiring and exact control assignments remain unverified.
  assembly-diagram.svg is a generated flat front projection of all 6,452 source
  triangles, using the same groups and custom colours. It is not an exact
  textured/logo rendering. It supports 2D assembly when WebGL is unavailable.
  Regenerate with node --import tsx scripts/prepare-electric-assembly.mjs.
