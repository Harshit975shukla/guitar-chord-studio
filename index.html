💡 How GuitarTuna Cancels Background Noise & What We Implemented:
1. Spectral Noise Subtraction: When you click "Calibrate Room Noise", the app takes a 2-second fingerprint of your fan, AC, or ambient hum. It then subtracts that exact noise footprint from every live microphone frame.
2. Hardware Bandpass Filtering: Web Audio Biquad filters cut off low room rumbles (< 75 Hz) and high-frequency clicks (> 1300 Hz), focusing exclusively on the 6 guitar strings.
3. Strum Onset Triggering (Attack Detection): Instead of constantly analyzing noisy air, the engine sleeps until you strike a chord. The sudden energy surge triggers analysis during the clean 150ms–400ms ringing sustain phase, then locks the result!
